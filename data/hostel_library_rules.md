# Hargrove Institute of Technology: Hostel and Library Regulations 2026 (fictional document)

## 1. Hostel Admission

1.1 Hostel rooms are allotted for one academic year at a time. Allotment lapses automatically on 31 July unless renewed.

1.2 Renewal applications must be submitted between 1 June and 20 June through the warden's office. Late applications incur a fee of Rs 500.

1.3 A security deposit of Rs 8,000 is collected at first admission and is refundable within 45 days of vacating the room, subject to deductions for damage.

## 2. Hostel Conduct

2.1 Hostel gates close at 10:30 pm on weekdays and 11:30 pm on Fridays and Saturdays.

2.2 Students returning after closing time must sign the late register at the gate. Three late entries in one semester lead to a written warning from the warden.

2.3 Visitors are permitted in the common room only between 4:00 pm and 7:00 pm. Visitors are not permitted in rooms under any circumstances.

2.4 Use of electric heaters, induction cooktops and immersion rods in rooms is prohibited. A confiscated appliance is returned only at the end of the semester.

## 3. Mess

3.1 Mess charges are Rs 4,200 per month, billed per semester in advance.

3.2 A student who will be absent for seven or more consecutive days may apply for a mess rebate of Rs 120 per day, provided the application reaches the mess committee at least two days before departure.

3.3 Rebates are not available for absences during examination periods.

## 4. Library Membership

4.1 Undergraduate students may borrow up to four books at a time for 14 days. Postgraduate students may borrow up to six books for 21 days.

4.2 One renewal of 7 days is permitted per book, provided no other member has reserved it.

4.3 The overdue fine is Rs 2 per day per book, capped at the purchase price of the book.

4.4 A lost book must be replaced with the same edition or paid for at the current price plus a processing charge of Rs 100.

## 5. Library Spaces

5.1 The reading hall is open from 8:00 am to 10:00 pm on working days and 9:00 am to 5:00 pm on Sundays.

5.2 During the examination fortnight, the reading hall remains open until midnight.

5.3 Group study rooms must be booked at the circulation desk and may be held for at most two hours per day per group.

5.4 Food and open beverages are not permitted inside the reading hall. Water in sealed bottles is allowed.

## 6. Grievances

6.1 Complaints about hostel or library services may be submitted in writing to the Dean of Student Welfare.

6.2 The Dean's office acknowledges a complaint within three working days.
